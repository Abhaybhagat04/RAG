Welcome to AuraRAG

An advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) agent that answers questions based solely on the documents you upload.

1. Upload PDF

Drag in research papers, resumes, manuals, or notes. ChromaDB will index them in chunks.

2. Ask Questions

Ask anything about the uploaded content. AuraRAG performs similarity searches to retrieve context.

3. Inspect Sources

Click "View Context Sources" under any response to see exact excerpts retrieved to build the answer.
